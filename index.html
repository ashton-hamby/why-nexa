A personal perspective

Why I chose NEXA

I believe there's power in numbers.

One of the biggest advantages of the mortgage broker model is the ability to offer clients options. But who you choose to partner with matters, too.

NEXA's size and wholesale lender relationships were important reasons behind my decision. I also have great professional relationships with Anthony Casa and Jimmy Hobson, both NEXA Executive Partners.

I didn't choose NEXA simply because of a compensation plan. I chose it because I believe in the opportunity to build something bigger, and I'd love to help other loan officers explore whether it's the right fit for them.

Beyond the transition

Support for the business you're building.

I work directly with producing loan officers as a Loan Partner, helping with the technical and operational work behind applications, pre-approvals, loan preparation, and closing. If you're looking for more capacity to originate, my services may be another part of the conversation.
